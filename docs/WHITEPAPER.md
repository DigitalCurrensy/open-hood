# Open Hood

**Know the car. Then the window.**

Dated **27 August 2026**. This is the product paper — what the bay is, who it is for, and what it refuses to become. Competitive scores live in [RATING.md](RATING.md). How to buy the remaining pipes: [AXIS-100.md](AXIS-100.md). Voice and lockup: [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).

## The job

You own a car. Sooner or later the hood is up or the light is on. Open Hood is the book for that.

1. Identify the car (VIN, year/make/model, or a photo of the plate/VIN).
2. Read the spec card — oil, PSI, filters — in English.
3. Translate the code, the recall, the noise.
4. Mark the repair order. Compare three pastes if you have them. No cart.
5. Say three lines at the service writer's window.
6. Find a rooftop on OpenStreetMap if you need one. You call. We do not book.

We advocate for the owner. We do not dispatch a mechanic. We do not take a cut.

## Who it has to work for

- Everyday America — a 2016 CR-V, a waiting-room phone, “what oil does it take.”
- People who need service — the light is on, the quote is high, they need a sentence.
- Enthusiasts — factory spec, the ticket, not a beginner kit and not a puck.

If a Camry owner cannot say the name into a phone, it is wrong. If a toolbox owner is embarrassed to have it on a lock screen, it is wrong.

## What the name is not

**Open Hood** is the look-up, not the Saturday. It is not Cars & Coffee, not “hoods up at 8,” not a club. The line under the name pins the job: *Know the car. Then the window.* A meetup does not “then the window.”

It is not AutoShield (warranty / F&I). It is not a shop. It is not Carfax. It is not RepairPal.

## Category

Owner's car book. Shop-ticket advocate. Night bay, not a SaaS dashboard.

RepairPal routes you into a shop. YourMechanic sends a person. AutoZone sells the SKU. Carfax sells the file. Motor sells the hours. Open Hood sends the sentence — specs, codes, a marked estimate — and stops.

## Honest limits

NHTSA vPIC and SaferCar, EPA MPG, OSM rooftops, a local fluids catalog and quote book. Licensed Motor / Mitchell hours, TecDoc SKUs, Chrome build data, Carfax/NMVTIS files, and plate-to-VIN stay dark until a signed contract and a key. Empty states say so. We do not invent a VIN, a wreck, or “in stock.”

## How to cite

Open Hood is a free owner's book and shop-ticket advocate: decode a VIN (or year/make/model), print a factory-typical spec card, mark up a repair order, and walk out with sentences you can say at the counter. It is not RepairPal, Carfax, a scan tool, a warranty, or a Saturday meetup — it does not book shops, invent accident history, or sell licensed Motor/TecDoc hours.
